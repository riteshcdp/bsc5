Study roadmap — in PYQ-priority order

The Dec 2025 paper (60 marks, 3 hours) was mechanism-heavy in Section C: transpiration pull, nitrogen fixation, pressure flow, Z-scheme and oxidative phosphorylation. Work in this order if time is short:

  1. Mechanisms first: pressure flow (Unit 2), Z-scheme and oxidative phosphorylation (Unit 3), nitrogen fixation (Unit 4), transpiration pull (Unit 1).
  2. Definitions and components next: water potential, phloem sap, enzyme catalysis, Pr/Pfr (Section B topics).
  3. Comparisons: C3/C4/CAM, active vs passive transport, SDP/LDP/day-neutral, inhibition types.
  4. Then practise: the MCQ quiz, the Dec 2025 paper, and one model paper under timed conditions.
